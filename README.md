Abstract — Energy efficiency in backbone network is a 
concern with the exponential increase of internet traffic, 
and energy proportional computing (where energy 
consumption scales with traffic) offers optimal solution to 
reducing energy waste.  This paper explores energy 
reduction techniques, analysing energy consumptions 
based on router states, such as idle, sleep, receive or 
transmit, and work on optimization strategies using 
NetSim simulations. Findings in this paper suggest energy 
proportional routing, and sleep scheduling can 
significantly reduce consumption without impacting the 
network performance.   
Key words: network energy consumption, routing states, 
NetSim Simultaion, energy proportional computing,
